APEX GLOBAL LOGISTICS LLC - ENTERPRISE VENDOR MANAGEMENT & INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Document Reference: AGL-POL-PROC-2026-04
Classification: Internal Corporate Policy & Guidelines
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

POLICY OVERVIEW & PURPOSE
This Vendor Management and Information Security Policy sets forth mandatory compliance requirements for all vendor contracts, master service agreements, and software-as-a-service (SaaS) procurement. All business units, procurement specialists, and contract administrators must ensure incoming agreements comply with these standards.

SECTION 1. CONTRACT TERM AND RENEWAL NOTICE REQUIREMENTS
1.1 Minimum Renewal Notice Period. Every contract that contains an automatic renewal or evergreen renewal clause must specify a non-renewal notice period of not less than sixty (60) calendar days prior to the expiration of the current term.
1.2 Policy Gap Escalation. Any vendor contract proposing a renewal notice period of thirty (30) days or fewer is strictly classified as a high commercial risk and must be escalated to the Director of Global Procurement for renegotiation before approval.

SECTION 2. VENDOR SECURITY & COMPLIANCE MANDATES
2.1 SOC 2 Type II Certification. All cloud service providers and vendors possessing, processing, or transmitting Apex corporate or customer data must maintain an active SOC 2 Type II certification covering the Security and Confidentiality Trust Services Criteria.
2.2 Penetration Testing. Vendors must perform independent third-party penetration testing at least once every twelve (12) calendar months and provide proof of testing and remediation upon request.

SECTION 3. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION WINDOWS
3.1 Incident Reporting Window. In the event of any confirmed or suspected data compromise, security incident, or unauthorized access, the vendor must notify Apex Global Logistics via email to soc@apexlogistics.com within forty-eight (48) hours of discovery. Agreements allowing longer reporting windows (e.g. 7 business days) are prohibited.

SECTION 4. PAYMENT TERMS AND AUDIT RIGHTS
4.1 Standard Payment Terms. The standard corporate payment term is Net 30 days from date of valid invoice receipt.
4.2 Audit Rights. Contracts with critical technology vendors must preserve Apex's right to request compliance and billing audit records upon reasonable advance notice.
